Controls

  • W A S D Move
  • Mouse Aim
  • Left Click Primary attack (hold to repeat)
  • Right Click Secondary ability
  • E / F Class abilities (E at a station or chest: use it)
  • Hold E By a downed teammate: revive them (multiplayer)
  • Q Ultimate (charges as you deal damage)
  • Shift Dodge (brief invulnerability)
  • 1 - 5 Drink the potion in that bag slot (buy potions at the shop)
  • Mouse on the bag Drag to move / stack potions, drag out of the bag to drop them (teammates can pick them up), right click to drink
  • Esc Pause
  • M Toggle music
  • Keys above are the defaults. Change them in Settings.

Red zones on the ground are enemy attacks winding up. Move!

Potion Guide

Buy potions from Mira in each stage's shop (prices shown are stage 1; they rise each stage). Your bag has 5 slots holding up to 10 of a kind. Drink with 1-5, or right click a slot. A timed effect can't be drunk again while it lasts - only a stronger tier (II, III) can go on over it.
