Controls

  • W A S D Move
  • Mouse Aim
  • Left Click Primary attack (hold to repeat)
  • Right Click Secondary ability
  • E / F Class abilities (E at a station or chest: use it)
  • Hold E By a downed teammate: revive them (multiplayer)
  • Q Ultimate (charges as you deal damage)
  • Shift Dodge (brief invulnerability)
  • 1 - 5 Drink the potion in that bag slot (buy potions at the shop)
  • H Quick heal: drink the strongest health potion in your bag
  • Mouse on the bag Drag to move / stack potions, drag out of the bag to drop them (teammates can pick them up), right click to drink
  • Esc Pause
  • M Toggle music
  • Keys above are the defaults. Change them in Settings.

Potion Guide

Buy potions from Mira in each stage's shop (prices shown are stage 1; they rise each stage). Your bag has 5 slots (7 with the Backpack upgrade) holding up to 10 of a kind. Drink with the number keys, or right click a slot. H drinks your strongest health potion. A timed effect can't be drunk again while it lasts - only a stronger tier (II, III) can go on over it.

SHOW

Enchantment Tome

Every enchantment Bram can bind to you in the dungeon shops. Each stage starts with one free pick; more can be bought at each stage's shop. Each can be owned once per run.
